## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Data Analysis Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to provide comprehensive business insights into sales performance, profitability, product performance, customer transactions, promotional effectiveness, and regional sales trends.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key business metrics, compare sales across different time periods, identify top-performing products, and make data-driven business decisions.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses generate thousands of sales transactions every year, making it difficult to manually identify trends, profitable products, customer purchasing behavior, and promotional effectiveness.

This dashboard answers critical business questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which products perform poorly?
- Which cities contribute the highest sales?
- How effective are promotional campaigns?
- How do sales compare between two different time periods?
- What is the relationship between Profit and Net Sales?
- How do sales trends change over time?

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Sales Overview Dashboard

This page provides an executive overview of business performance.

### Key KPIs

- Total Number of Orders
- Sales by City
- Average Discount by Promotion Category
- Profit vs Net Sales
- Sales Trend Over Time

### Insights

- Sales are distributed across multiple Indian cities.
- Weekend Flash Sales generated the highest average discount.
- Profit shows a strong positive relationship with Net Sales.
- Sales demonstrate consistent growth with seasonal fluctuations.

---

## 2️⃣ Product Performance Dashboard

This page analyzes product performance across different business metrics.

### Visualizations

- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Bottom 5 Products by Sales
- Top 5 Products by Quantity Sold
- Bottom 5 Products by Quantity Sold
- Top 5 Products by Profit
- Bottom 5 Products by Profit

### Insights

- Apple iPhone 14 generated the highest sales and profit.
- Premium electronic products contribute significantly to revenue.
- Some products generate high sales but relatively lower profits.
- Underperforming products can be reviewed for pricing or promotional improvements.

---

## 3️⃣ Sales Comparison Dashboard

This dashboard compares business performance between two different date ranges.

### Metrics

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold

### Features

- Interactive Date Filters
- Period-to-Period Comparison
- Dynamic KPI Cards

### Insights

- Easily compare overall business performance between selected periods.
- Identify growth or decline in revenue and profitability.

---

## 4️⃣ Time Period Performance Dashboard

This page enables detailed comparison between custom date ranges.

### Analysis Includes

- Sales Comparison
- Profit Comparison
- Quantity Comparison

### Business Value

- Supports year-over-year analysis.
- Helps evaluate business growth.
- Measures promotional campaign effectiveness.
- Enables strategic planning.
